Human Testing Lab

A public testing surface for builders who need real people, real devices and real confusion — not only automated checks.

Integration prototype · core service not live on this deployment yet

Test something

Claim a bounded test, follow the requested tasks, record what actually happened and earn Test Minutes when the creator accepts useful evidence.

Get tested

Publish a test against your own project, reserve Test Minutes and specify exactly what testers may and may not do.

Evidence, not praise

Results are task outcomes, expected versus actual behaviour, notes and optional screenshots or recordings — not a popularity contest.

System boundary

Rinkimirikata is the public human-facing surface. Human Test Network owns tests, claims, evidence and Test Minutes. Omniforge/M8 can prepare structured tests and consume the resulting evidence for Stress Test/Reforge workflows.

This page is intentionally labelled as an integration prototype until the Human Test Network API is deployed and connected. It does not fake a live tester marketplace.
