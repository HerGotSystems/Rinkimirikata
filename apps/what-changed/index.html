An Omnunity crossing

This is not a personality test and it does not assign an ideology. It shows five human needs twice, with one identifying detail changed, then compares only your own answers.

Your personal mirror is calculated inside this browser. No account, name, email, advertising identifier, precise location, or third-party analytics are used.

After a completed crossing, anonymous totals may be updated. The database stores counters only, not a row containing your individual crossing.

The people and groups inside the crossing are fictional. They are constructed to expose how labels can affect judgement, not to describe real communities.
