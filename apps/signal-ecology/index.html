01

The normalization problem that isn't a problem

A text string, a prime number sequence, a microphone feed, an image row, the Planck blackbody radiation spectrum. These appear to be fundamentally incompatible sources. They are not.

Map each to a normalized array of 512 floating-point values in [0, 1]. At that resolution, the ontological difference between them dissolves. A chord progression and a Lorenz attractor become indistinguishable to an operator.

"The source ontology is permanently out of scope."

Once signal unification is in place, the rest of the system operates on a single assumption: any two signals can be operated on, scored, mutated, persisted, and evolved. This is the foundational move of Signal Ecology: everything becomes signal.

Beyond generation

This loop is borrowed from artificial life. Karl Sims ran the same loop in 1994 for evolved virtual creatures. The loop was the invention — not the physics, not the rendering. The loop made discovery cumulative rather than ephemeral.

Signal Ecology applies the same loop to audiovisual signal states in a browser, with human aesthetic judgment replacing the fitness function.

03

The corpus is the invention

The renderer is not the invention. The audio engine is not the invention. The equations are not the invention.

"The corpus turns ephemeral generation into ecological exploration."

A corpus of scored, timestamped, operator-chained signal configurations is not a gallery. A gallery holds finished work. A corpus holds discovered states — including the ambiguous, the failed, the interesting-but-not-quite. The corpus is the memory of an exploration, not the exhibition of its results.

Without corpus persistence, a mutation system is a toy. With persistence: evolution becomes meaningful, scoring becomes comparative, mutation becomes cumulative, exploration becomes navigable, discovery becomes social.

04

Cultivation, not construction

Hydra requires code. Strudel requires patterns. TouchDesigner requires node graphs. These are construction models: the operator builds the system, then observes it.

Signal Ecology operates on a different model. The operator does not construct the system. The operator selects from what the system generates, steers the evolution of what survives, and cultivates an ecology of discovered states over time. Authorship is real — selection pressure is a creative act — but it operates through judgment rather than construction.

More gardener than programmer. More ecologist than engineer.

The no-code constraint is therefore philosophical, not a simplification. Removing the construction barrier is what makes cultivation possible.

06

Collective emergence archaeology

A corpus stored locally is a personal ecosystem. A corpus that can be published, forked, browsed, and merged becomes something else entirely.

GitHub proved this model for code: commits, diffs, forks, branches. The power was not storage — it was that evolutionary relationships became navigable. You could trace any line's history. You could fork at any point. You could see what was tried and abandoned.

Signal Ecology corpora have the same structural properties: mutation lineage, rejection history, scoring pressure over time, emergent states as restorable configurations — not images, but full signal environments. When corpora are public, the system gains collective emergence archaeology.

Not authored by any one person. Shaped by the cumulative selection pressure of everyone who cultivated it.

07

The term, offered as infrastructure

Signal Ecology describes a practice, not a medium. It does not specify what signals contain, what operators do, or what rendering looks like. It specifies only:

Signal unification as the foundational architecture. The generate→mutate→evaluate→preserve→branch→evolve loop as the operational model. The corpus as persistent memory of discovered complexity configurations. Cultivation as the authorship mode.

"The term is offered as a category, not a brand. If other practitioners build systems with these properties, they should call them Signal Ecology systems."

Categories form when more than one person uses the term for more than one thing. Until then, CDME is the first implementation of a nameable practice.

REF

CDME — Cross-Domain Mutation Engine

CDME is the reference implementation of Signal Ecology. Browser-native, single-file, no dependencies, no account required. Sources span physics constants, mathematical equations, audio input, image data, and text. Operators combine any signal with any other. The emergence scorer runs continuously. The corpus persists locally and exports as JSON.

The AIP Protocol (CC BY 4.0, HerGotSystems) defines the open interchange format for Signal Ecology configurations — the spec that makes corpora portable across implementations.
