Guide

RINKIMIRIKATA is a public playground for tools, visual systems, music experiments, art generators, learning engines, belief engines, strange ideas and unfinished machines.

How to use this place

  • Open Apps to try browser tools.
  • Open Projects to see bigger systems and source links.
  • Open Lab for rough experiments and unstable tests.
  • Open EMVY CHECK for music.

What to expect

  • Some tools are polished enough to use.
  • Some tools are prototypes.
  • Some things may be weird, broken, unfinished or experimental.
  • Status labels are honest on purpose.

Your files and privacy

Most tools are designed to work browser-local where possible. That means if you load a local MP3, CSV, image or text file, it should stay on your device unless the page clearly says it uploads something.

Do not upload private, dangerous, sensitive or legally risky files into random experiments unless you understand what the tool does.

Be decent

Use the tools, enjoy the weirdness, test things, break things by accident, report bugs, give feedback, share ideas. Do not abuse, spam, harass, steal credit, attack the site, or be pointlessly rude.

Sharp criticism is fine. Useful bug reports are welcome. Being a dick is boring.

Feedback

If something works, breaks, confuses you, inspires you, annoys you, or gives you an idea, send feedback.
